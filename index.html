What we are building

A farm that is useful before it is impressive.

We are developing KettleFarms in layers: protect what already works, improve soil and water systems, produce safe food, measure outcomes, then add experiences and technology where they genuinely help.

01

KettleFarms Produce

Grapes, seasonal vegetables, fruits, eggs and food for our own home before scaling direct sales.

02

KettleFarms Experience

A peaceful working farm first. Visits, workshops and stays only when the farm itself is ready.

03

KettleFarms Lab

JADAM-inspired trials, soil and water data, IoT sensors, irrigation monitoring and transparent farm economics.

Our approach

Low input. High observation.

Our goal is not to buy every new agricultural technology. It is to understand the land, reduce unnecessary inputs and use technology only when it solves a real problem.
